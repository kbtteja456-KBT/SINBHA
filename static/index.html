🌸 Terms of Service Needs Legal Review

1. AI Companion & Boundaries: SINBHA is an automated AI personal assistant designed to help you organize daily tasks, habits, and gentle reminders. SINBHA is NOT a licensed therapist, healthcare professional, or emergency crisis service. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please immediately call 988 or your local emergency services.

2. Age Requirement (18+): You must be at least 18 years of age to register and use SINBHA.

3. AI Provider Processing: Your chat messages are processed by an AI provider (e.g. OpenRouter / Google Gemini) to generate conversational responses and extract schedule details.

4. Account Security: You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials with a strong password (minimum 8 characters).

🌸 Privacy Policy Needs Legal Review

1. Strict User Isolation: Every message, fact, task, and reminder is bound to your user account and never shared with other users. Admins cannot read your chat contents.

2. Encryption at Rest: Sensitive message contents and memory facts are stored encrypted at rest in our database using Fernet symmetric encryption.

3. AI Processing: Messages are processed by third-party AI models to interpret tasks and reply warmly. Plain passwords and tokens are never logged.

4. Data Rights: You can download a complete JSON archive of all your data (Settings → Download My Data) or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.
